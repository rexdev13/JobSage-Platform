## Scope and limits

- The route pass checked 15 Technology and 9 Engineering sites that had high- or medium-confidence website identity in the preceding website pilot.
- The source cohort comes from the sponsor export last modified **26 September 2026**. It does not represent all Technology or Engineering employers.
- Web search was disabled and produced zero queries. The pass used first-party website checks, local support leads, and SponsorList; it was not a broad web search.
- The run made 141 public HTTP attempts, with no retries, cache hits, robots/policy blocks, or employer-identity rejections.
- Findings are recommendations for human review only. Nothing was imported, and no database or production data was queried or changed.
