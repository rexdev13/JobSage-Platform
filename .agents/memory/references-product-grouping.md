---
name: References product grouping
description: Owner requirement to keep candidate references under CV and supporting documents
---

Candidate References belong inside “CV and Supporting Documents,” not as a separate
top-level or sidebar product destination.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested that references be grouped with CVs,
qualifications and supporting evidence rather than treated as something separate.

**How to apply:** Preserve this grouping in future web/mobile navigation work.
Moving the interface does not mean flattening employer-verified reference records
into ordinary uploaded files or removing their existing verification behavior.
