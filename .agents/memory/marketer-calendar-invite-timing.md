---
name: Marketer calendar invite timing
description: Preserve marketer-controlled timing for invitations on fixed-time Google Meet calls.
---

For marketer-created fixed-time Google Meet events, create the event on the marketer's calendar without adding the lead as an attendee or sending notifications. The marketer explicitly sends the lead invitation later; that action adds the lead through the owning marketer's Google Calendar connection. Keep public self-booking and Calendly behavior independent.

**Why:** The product owner selected delayed, marketer-controlled sending so leads are not invited before the marketer is ready.

**How to apply:** Preserve the attendee-free initial event and explicit invite action when changing scheduled-call creation, attendee updates, or calendar notifications. Test both stages.
