---
name: Professional strengths drafting boundaries
description: Product and privacy decisions for CV-backed professional-strength drafts.
---

Strength drafting is a lightweight profile-writing action, not a full CV rewrite, and must have an independent allowance.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested Word/scanned-CV support and a separate quota so writing strengths does not consume full CV rewrites.

**How to apply:** Preserve the separate allowance when changing drafting or CV-writing workflows. Keep candidates' existing text intact until they choose to append or replace it, then explicitly save.

Scanned CVs are OCRed locally; only sanitised extracted text goes to the AI provider, not raw page images.

**Why:** Supporting scanned CVs should not increase disclosure of candidates' original documents to the model provider. OCR can also misread facts, so source excerpts and the OCR notice remain important.

**How to apply:** Preserve the review gate and source excerpts. Do not silently turn OCR output into confirmed candidate facts.
