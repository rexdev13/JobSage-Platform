Do not reuse the detached two-piece J as the mobile launcher icon or restore the near-black typed-JOBSAGE-and-spinner loading overlay. Preserve the supplied full company wordmark and exact tagline in normal branding.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the detached mark's Android appearance and described the black logo-and-spinner loading screen as disconcerting. Company wordmark approval did not imply approval of that launcher adaptation.

**How to apply:** Keep mobile launcher artwork cohesive and readable under Android masks. Separate OS launch screens from the in-app network-loading state. Expo Go cannot establish the appearance of installed native icons or release splash screens; report native-build verification limits explicitly.
