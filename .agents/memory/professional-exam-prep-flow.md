---
name: Professional exam-prep flow
description: Product scope for integrating professional exam preparation into the candidate journey.
---

Remove the standalone Learning Hub page and candidate-facing link, but retain its learning capability as contextual exam-preparation support. Make it available only to doctors, nurses, and dentists. Advertise it as support to prepare for professional examinations without promising a pass. Integrate it into relevant journey stages without blocking eligibility, applications, or progress. Keep staff administration of learning resources separate.

**Why:** The user wants the learning feature to directly support exam outcomes for these three professions while remaining useful and non-coercive throughout the wider candidate flow.

**How to apply:** Gate both candidate UI and learning-content access by normalized saved profession. Include optional support on the candidate dashboard and career journey, plus exam/registration guidance; make prompts dismissible and never require completion to continue. Preserve staff-only resource management.
