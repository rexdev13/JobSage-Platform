# Required owner and legal review before release

Status: **release gate; review has not been completed.** This checklist records choices and evidence required; it is not legal advice or owner approval.

## Owner-controlled facts

- Legal operating entity, registration/address details and the monitored public privacy/support contact.
- Governing-law and jurisdiction clauses; legal venue; complaint/escalation contact.
- Company financial-year end used to calculate the approved six-year financial retention period.
- Approved iOS bundle ID, Android application ID, developer/store ownership and EAS project/account.
- Distribution regions, App Store category, Google Play category, listing claims, screenshot approvals and review/test accounts.
- Production website domain and public policy/support URLs to inject as the native app's first-party origin.
- EULA choice (standard Apple EULA or a reviewed custom agreement).

## Counsel/privacy/processor evidence

- Validate Privacy/Terms text, data-processing role, lawful bases, sensitive-data conditions, consent/version reacceptance, international transfers and all retention exceptions.
- Verify Replit, OpenAI via Replit AI Integrations, Resend, Calendly, payment (if added), analytics (if added), app stores, Google Calendar and every SDK/subprocessor contract, region, retention, deletion and incident process.
- Reconcile the Apple privacy label and Google Data Safety declarations to the signed apps and current controlled WebView.
- Decide Apple/Google payments/IAP treatment for Readiness upgrades, existing account entitlements and any allowed storefront-specific external purchase program.
- Approve audience/content ratings, AI disclosures, UGC terms/report/block surfaces, moderation staffing and escalation/appeal response objectives.
- Approve a privacy manifest, required-reason API declarations, OS permission descriptions, file/camera flows and the native privacy/permission placements.
- Confirm the public deletion web resource actually accepts and completes eligible requests before declaring it functional; verify 30-day active deletion and no-more-than-90-day encrypted backup expiry with restore replay.
- Approve operational access for data requests and deletion completion, provider escalation, security/complaint handling and moderation queues.

## Evidence bar

Legal, processor, payment, deletion, restore and store statements remain **NOT COMPLETE** until approved and backed by direct evidence. A source file, test fixture or unit test does not substitute for a signed app, provider result, device run or store-console review.
