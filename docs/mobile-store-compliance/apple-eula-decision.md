# Apple EULA decision record

Status: **owner/legal decision required; no EULA choice is asserted.**

## Current evidence

- JOBSAGE has a public Terms of Service at `/terms`, linked during candidate and employer registration.
- No custom Apple EULA text or App Store Connect EULA configuration was identified in the repository.
- Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement is available at [Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/). A custom agreement would need to satisfy Apple's minimum terms.

## Decision to record

The owner and qualified counsel must choose one before submission:

- [ ] Use Apple's standard EULA and verify that it is appropriate for the approved operator and app distribution.
- [ ] Submit a legally reviewed custom EULA and provide its exact text and distribution configuration.
- [ ] Confirm another documented store-supported arrangement with counsel.

The web Terms of Service and the iOS EULA serve different interfaces/purposes; this draft does not declare them equivalent or replace either. Do not create a custom legal agreement, operator identity, governing-law clause or effective date in this file.
