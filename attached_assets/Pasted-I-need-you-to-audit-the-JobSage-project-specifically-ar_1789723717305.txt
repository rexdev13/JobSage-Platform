2. COMPANY WEBSITES

Once a vacancy exists in JobSage, users can either:

* Apply through the original application route, OR
* Send their CV for that vacancy through JobSage.

"Send CV" is NOT a vacancy source. It is an application method for vacancies we have already discovered.

JOB SAGE MUST SHOW REAL, CURRENT, OPEN VACANCIES.
