Scope
- Every route, page, modal, drawer, dropdown, table, form, nav, footer, and empty/error/loading state.

What “broken” means — fix every instance
- Horizontal page scroll caused by content wider than the viewport (100vw mistakes, fixed widths, negative margins, long unbroken strings, images, iframes, code blocks).
- Text, buttons, inputs, or cards cut off, overflowing, or overlapping.
- Tap targets smaller than 44×44px, or controls jammed together.
- Nav that does not collapse to a usable mobile menu, or a menu that cannot be closed, traps scroll, or sits under other content.
- Fixed/sticky headers, footers, chat widgets, or cookie bars that cover content or inputs. When a field is focused, it must stay visible above the keyboard.
- Multi-column layouts that stay side-by-side when they should stack. Tables that cannot be read: wrap them in a horizontal scroller with a visible cue, or restack rows on small screens. Do not leave a table that blows out the page.
- Images, video, and iframes without max-width: 100% and height: auto (or an aspect-ratio box).
- Font sizes below 16px on form inputs (iOS zoom). Body text that is unreadable or line-length that is fine only on desktop.
- Modals, sheets, and popovers that are wider than the screen, cannot scroll, or whose close button is off-screen.
- Flex/grid children with min-width: auto that refuse to shrink. Add min-width: 0 / overflow handling where needed.
- Hover-only interactions with no tap equivalent.
- Safe-area insets ignored on notched phones (padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom) on fixed bars).

How to work
1. Inventory every route and shared layout component before changing anything. List them.
2. Open the running app and check each route at 375px and 320px. Screenshot or describe the actual failure before editing.
3. Fix using the project’s existing approach (Tailwind breakpoints, CSS modules, styled-components, etc.). Prefer mobile-first. Do not add a second conflicting stylesheet.
4. Use relative units and existing spacing/type tokens. Avoid new fixed pixel widths for layout.
5. After each cluster of fixes, recheck that route at 320, 375, and 1280. Then move to the next route.
6. When two pages share a component, fix the component and recheck both pages.
