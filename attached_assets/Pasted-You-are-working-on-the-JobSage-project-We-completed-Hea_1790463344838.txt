Unverified:
- no reliable source
- identity mismatch
- generic directory only
- unreachable/blocked
- employer not clearly tied to sponsor

Confidence:
- `high`: employer identity clear, source is official/recognized, name/location/domain match is strong.
- `medium`: likely correct, but one piece of evidence is weaker.
- `low`: possible match, manual review required.
- `unverified`: no trustworthy match.
