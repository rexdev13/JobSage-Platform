#455 - Reliable extension activation and dismissable launcher
What & Why
The JOBSAGE Smart Apply extension should reliably follow candidates onto external job sites and stay out of their way when unwanted. Today activation depends on ?ref=jobsage surviving in the URL at page load (redirects and in-site navigation kill it), and the floating launcher is fixed in the bottom-right corner with no way to move or dismiss it.

Done looks like
Clicking an apply link from JOBSAGE activates the sidebar on the employer's site even when the job board strips the ?ref= parameter during redirect, and activation persists as the candidate navigates through multi-page application flows in the same tab.
The sidebar does NOT appear on sites the candidate visited independently (no false activations).
The launcher icon can be dragged to any screen edge/position, and its position is remembered.
The candidate can fully dismiss the launcher (an explicit close/hide affordance), with sensible scope — e.g. hide for this site or this session — and a way to bring it back from the extension popup.
On pages where the extension has nothing useful to offer (unrecognized site, no application form detected), the launcher stays hidden or minimal instead of showing the badge.
Behavior is verified in a real Chrome install against at least NHS Jobs and Trac, including a redirect that drops query parameters.
Out of scope
Adding SAGE chat to the extension sidebar (separate future work).
Rich-text ATS question detection (covered by Task #408).
Automating the downloadable zip refresh (covered by Task #399).
Firefox/Edge support.
Steps
Persist activation across redirects and navigation — Track outbound apply clicks so activation survives ?ref= stripping: mark the tab as JOBSAGE-activated in the background service worker (or session storage keyed to the tab) when the candidate leaves via a tagged link, and let the content script consult that mark instead of relying only on the URL parameter. Expire the mark when the tab moves to a clearly unrelated domain so independent browsing never triggers the sidebar.
Movable launcher — Make the floating pill/badge draggable within the viewport and persist its chosen position (per device via extension storage), restoring it on subsequent pages.
Dismissal and suppression — Add an explicit dismiss affordance on the launcher with a hide-for-this-site and/or hide-for-this-session choice, persisted in extension storage; surface a re-enable control in the extension popup. Tighten the "minimal" behavior so the launcher is hidden (not just faded) on pages with no recognized job board and no detected application form.
Manifest/permission updates — Add whatever permissions the tab-tracking approach needs (e.g. tabs/webNavigation) and bump the extension version.
Real-browser verification — Load the built extension unpacked in Chrome and verify: activation via a param-stripping redirect, persistence across a multi-page Trac/NHS Jobs application, drag + position persistence, dismissal and re-enable from the popup, and no activation on an independently visited site.
