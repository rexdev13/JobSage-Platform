2) Company-site pipeline: company_site + company_site_probe
3) Liveness / contact only if they affect vacancy visibility — mention briefly, don’t deep-dive

Report structure (use these exact sections)

A. Executive snapshot (10 lines max)
- Total open/active vacancies (define your filter: not closed, within TTL, etc.)

- Professions/sectors with strong coverage vs near-empty
