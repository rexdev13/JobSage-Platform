PHASE 2 — COMPLETE ACCOUNT DELETION

Implement a central account-deletion orchestrator. It must not be a collection of disconnected adapters.

The complete flow must:

- Require reauthentication and explicit confirmation.
- Create a persistent deletion request with statuses such as requested, processing, waiting_on_provider, completed, partially_failed and legally_restricted.
- Immediately revoke sessions, refresh tokens, device tokens and Sign in with Apple tokens where applicable.
- Stop marketing, notifications and automated applications immediately.
- Be idempotent and safely retryable.
- Delete active user-owned personal data within 30 calendar days.
- Track each database, file-storage and provider cleanup step independently.
- Retry temporary provider failures.
- Record non-sensitive completion evidence.
- Notify the user when deletion finishes.
- Never log deleted personal content or credentials.

Delete user-owned information including profiles, CVs, cover letters, uploaded documents, identity documents, application drafts, saved items, preferences, Smart Apply data, AI content, attachments, device identifiers, temporary files and search-index entries.

Implement the approved shared-record rules:

- Candidate personal data must be deleted or anonymised in JOBSAGE-controlled records.
- Employer job listings and non-personal aggregate statistics may remain.
- Employer/staff account deletion must not automatically delete an organisation.
- Remove or anonymise the departing staff identity in shared history.
- Transfer shared ownership to another organisation administrator.
- A sole organisation owner must transfer ownership or use a separate organisation-deletion flow.
- Data already independently downloaded or received by an external employer or ATS cannot be recalled, but JOBSAGE-controlled copies must be removed.
- Show this limitation clearly to the user.

Implement legal-retention handling:

- Financial, tax, payment and invoice records: retain for six years from the relevant financial-year end where applicable.
- Security and fraud logs: retain for 12 months unless an active investigation requires longer.
- Closed complaint/dispute records: retain for 24 months unless needed for an active legal claim.
- Legal-hold or regulatory records: retain only for the documented obligation.
- Separate retained records from normal application data and block their use for marketing, recommendations or profiling.
- Keep only a minimal pseudonymous deletion receipt.
- Do not retain raw CVs, identity documents, passwords, tokens or AI content as deletion evidence.

Do not enable production deletion until the inventory, automated tests and staging verification pass. The feature may be enabled for fixtures and designated staging test accounts.

PHASE 3 — BACKUPS AND RESTORE SAFETY

Implement and document:

- Maximum 90-day encrypted backup expiry for deleted personal information.
- Deleted backup data placed beyond normal operational use.
- Deletion tombstones or an equivalent durable mechanism.
- Automatic deletion replay after any restore.
- A staging restore drill proving that a previously deleted test user is not resurrected.
- Provider retry/reconciliation after restore.
- Monitoring and alerts for failed deletion jobs.
- An operations runbook for restoring backups without reviving deleted accounts.

A unit test alone does not prove restore safety. Provide the staging procedure, timestamped result and database/file/provider checks. If the environment cannot perform an actual restore, mark that requirement as externally blocked rather than passed.
