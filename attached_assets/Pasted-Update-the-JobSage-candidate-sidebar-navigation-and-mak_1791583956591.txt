Mobile responsiveness

The sidebar must work cleanly from 320px-wide phones upward.

- Desktop at md and above:
  - Keep a persistent left sidebar.
  - Use a sensible width around 256–288px.
  - Ensure long labels wrap cleanly without clipping.
  - The main page must retain its remaining width and must not overflow horizontally.

- Mobile below md:
  - Open the navigation from the existing hamburger button as a left-side drawer.
  - Drawer width: approximately 88vw, with a maximum width of 320px.
  - Use 100dvh and account for device safe-area insets.
  - Prevent background-page scrolling while open.
  - Keep the drawer itself vertically scrollable.
  - Provide a clearly visible close button with at least a 44×44px touch target.
  - Navigation rows must have at least a 44px touch target.
  - Close when a working navigation item is selected.
  - Close when the backdrop is tapped.
  - Close when Escape is pressed.
  - Trap keyboard focus while open and return focus to the hamburger button when closed.
  - Add correct aria-label, aria-expanded, aria-controls, dialog title, and navigation landmark semantics.
  - Do not allow the drawer, backdrop, or close button to conflict with the impersonation banner.
  - Avoid horizontal overflow at 320px, 375px, 390px, 768px, and desktop widths.
