### THE 24-POINT MASTER AUDIT ITEMS
You must search the codebase and evaluate every single item below:
1. **Speculative Outreach Tracking:** Speculative CVs sent must trigger an inbox notification in JobSage confirming who it was sent to for follow-up.
2. **AI Copilot & Autofill Integration:** An AI assistant available as a floating chatbot that also integrates when applying on company websites to instantly populate/pre-fill candidate responses based on profile data (with a mandatory candidate review disclaimer).
3. **High-Accuracy CV Parsing:** AI parsing must correctly interpret academic vs. clinical qualifications without misrepresenting candidates (e.g., distinguishing a PhD in Education from a Medical Doctor).
4. **Sponsor Vacancy Refresher:** A "Check Vacancies" button at the top of sponsor licence lists to scrape/refresh sector roles, displaying total vacancy counts at the top and individual counts next to each company.
5. **Per-Company "Check Best Fit" Ranking:** A button next to sponsor companies displaying vacancies ranked from 100% to 0% fit with percentage match badges based on candidate document alignment.
6. **Unified "Send CV" & AI Cover Letters:** Speculative "Send CV" buttons must operate like "Apply with JobSage"—generating an AI cover letter and sending from the candidate's `@mail.jobsage.app` mailbox (not personal email).
7. **External Company Website Cookie Tracking:** Cookie tracking for direct website applications that routes confirmation and employer replies directly into the JobSage inbox and Tracked Applications tab.
8. **Comprehensive Sponsor Directory Display:** Sponsor companies must display their email address, phone number, physical address, and website.
9. **Universal `@mail.jobsage.app` Mailbox Provisioning:** Automatic creation of an internal JobSage email upon account creation, routing all employer communications away from personal accounts.
10. **Categorized Application Tracking:** Tracked applications must clearly separate and categorize direct job applications vs. speculative CV outreach.
11. **CV Personal Data Masking:** Outbound CVs must automatically mask candidate personal email addresses and phone numbers, replacing them with the JobSage alias.
12. **Cookie-Tracked Interview Pipeline:** Tracking interview invitations received in the JobSage inbox via cookies, updating tracking statuses, and rendering dashboard metrics automatically.
13. **Unfiltered 100%-to-0% Opportunities Feed:** Job opportunities must list all vacancies sorted from 100% to 0% fit without hard eligibility filters blocking them, displaying inline employer contact details.
14. **Sponsor Contact Data Completeness:** Explicit verification that all sponsor licence rows expose website, phone, email, and address fields.
15. **Prioritized Application Guidance (Top 5 / Next 5):** The job feed must visually recommend where candidates should apply first (Top 5 most fitting roles, followed by the Next 5).
16. **Standalone Regulatory & Visa Eligibility Module:** Dedicated eligibility checks based on official UK government guidance (PLAB, OSCE, QTS, Advance HE Fellowship) separating visa status from professional registration.
17. **Engaging Visual User Journey:** A concise, visually motivating user journey culminating in a strong goal representation (e.g., trophy or candidate in a dream office).
18. **Visual "Island" Progression Pipeline:** Numbered, actionable progression steps (Step 1, Step 2 -> Verification -> Dream Job) represented as visual islands getting closer to the goal.
19. **Real-Time Cross-Platform State Sync:** Toggling applications, bookmarks, or profile data must stay 100% synchronized across Dashboard, Opportunities, and Tracked Applications without refreshing.
20. **Consistent Multi-CV Management:** CV documents must be consistently managed, labeled, and aligned across all features.
21. **Granular Multi-CV Application Mapping:** The platform must clearly display which specific CV version was applied to which specific job or employer.
22. **UK-Standard Professional CV Generator:** Dynamically generated CVs must follow strict UK formatting standards, look highly professional, and embed the JobSage email alias.
23. **Next-Step AI Career Advisor:** An AI advisory engine that constantly reviews candidate application results and recommends concrete next steps.
24. **Long-Term Career Development & LinkedIn Export:** Features positioning the platform for long-term career growth, document archiving, and LinkedIn profile/milestone sharing.
